# Asset identity and capacity accounting — methods working draft

Unique database keys were not taken as proof of distinct physical generating units. We retain the frozen inventory and all source unit identifiers, while maintaining a separate, dated adjudication of aliases. Automatic name or spatial similarity is used only to prioritize review; it cannot authorize a merge. Approved research groups must retain provenance and agree on unit label, capacity, technology, fuel, heat classification, vintage, status, province and investment role. Conflicts require further review.

Two Funing project entries each describe two 100 MW combined-cycle CHP units commissioned in 2022. Their database pages cite the same government project article despite a government-domain migration. An operator announcement describes a two-unit project and the January/May 2022 commissioning sequence ([CNOOC Gas & Power](https://gaspower.cnooc.com.cn/xwdt/qdyw/202501/t20250107_110431.html)). Together with matching Chinese aliases, these observations support our research adjudication that the entries represent aliases of the same assets. Different owner aggregation and exact/approximate coordinates are retained as metadata conflicts. This is an inference, not an official database correction or serial-number-level field verification.

Applying only this adjudication maps 109 source records (24,959 MW) to 107 candidate assets (24,759 MW). The 200 MW difference is a duplicate-counting correction, not retirement, an estimate of available power, or a proportional correction to system benefits. The remaining inventory has not passed a complete physical identity audit. Neither this procedure nor the project announcements supply heat-service curves, outages, ramp limits, net-meter boundaries or survival to 2030. All asset candidates remain excluded from empirical dispatch admission until those requirements are resolved or explicitly bounded within the study design.

The implementation is checked against independently reconstructed equivalence components and once-per-asset capacity sums; provenance coverage, ordering invariance, incompatible merges, source reuse and prospective investment separation are also checked. These checks verify bookkeeping, not the truth of the underlying identity inference. Frozen source hashes, database revision identifiers and rejected source accesses are recorded separately from the interpretation.
