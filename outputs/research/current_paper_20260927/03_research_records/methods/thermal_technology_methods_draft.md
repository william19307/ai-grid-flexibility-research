# Working methods: separating unit identity, physical technology and investment role

Status: implemented as source-preserving input staging, not a dispatch-ready provincial fleet. The legacy 2030 membership is a conditional inventory selection, not observed 2030 capacity.

Each existing record retains its GEM unit ID, original labels, capacity, chronology and fuel/use metadata. Cycle technology does not establish the feasible operating region: a combined-cycle label is compatible with different heat obligations, and an unknown CHP flag is not a zero heat requirement. Industrial by-product steam generation receives no natural-gas price or intensity by default. Stable IDs remain primary keys when display names are ambiguous.

Installed units and prospective investments are separate records. Correcting the running cost of an existing CCGT must not automatically replace the technology, cost or permission of a future OCGT option. The archive's annual-average efficiencies, fuel price/intensity, VOM, investment, FOM and lifetime remain conditional references with year, unit and source descriptions. Heat coefficients are preserved as metadata; they are not applied until the unit-specific heat technology, temperature and feasible region are supported. The official [PyPSA extraction-condensing example](https://docs.pypsa.org/v1.0.0/examples/power-to-gas-boiler-chp/) illustrates the explicit heat-power region, and [PyPSA-Eur's technology description](https://pypsa-eur.readthedocs.io/en/v2025.01.0/supply_demand.html) distinguishes backpressure from extraction assumptions. Neither supplies parameters for these Chinese units.

For the archive-only constant-efficiency illustration, `m = fuel_price / efficiency + VOM`. At explicitly assumed discount rate r and lifetime n, `CRF = r / (1 - (1+r)^(-n))`, with limit `1/n` at r=0. Annual capital plus fixed O&M is `1000 * investment_EUR_per_kW * (CRF + FOM_percent/100)`. Equating this cost plus `m*h` across two new-build technologies gives a screening intersection; it does not optimize actual CHP dispatch, reliability or Chinese investment.

Source-qualified classification and source-qualified operation are different stages. The current manifest refuses empirical fleet compilation for all 109 records because heat, fuel/meter, availability, chronology and/or industrial boundaries remain unresolved. It also refuses edited readiness flags as a substitute for an independently reviewed operational compiler. This restriction does not enforce other legacy scripts; it defines this staging interface only.
